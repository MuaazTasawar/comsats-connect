COMSATS Connect

Messaging for classes, courses, departments and offices at COMSATS University Islamabad.

  • Groups are members-only You only see, read and receive messages from the groups you have been added to.
  • Notice groups are read-only Department announcements and Exam Cell notices are posted by faculty and admins. Students can read them.
  • Direct messages have limits Students message people they share a group with, or people who messaged them first.

Sign in

Students use their registration number. Faculty and staff use their username.
